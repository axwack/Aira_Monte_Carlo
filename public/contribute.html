Contribute And Check The Math

AiRA is built by one person, in public. The code is open, so anyone can check it. Here is where help is most useful.

Check The Math

This is the most valuable help. If you are a tax pro, a planner, or just careful with numbers, look at these and tell me what is wrong.

  • Tax brackets and state tables: src/data/stateBrackets.js
  • Medicare IRMAA tiers: src/data/irmaa2026.js
  • Required minimum distribution divisors: src/data/jointRmdTable.js and src/engine/rulesEngine.js
  • Withdrawal order and taxes: src/engine/buildWithdrawalWaterfall.js
  • Roth conversion plan: src/engine/rothConversionPlan.js

How to report a wrong number. Email tiredtoretire@gmail.com with the screen, the figure you see, the figure you expect, and the rule or source behind it. A precise report gets a fix and a thank-you in the app, under the handle you choose.

Read The Code

The source is on GitHub. The planner runs in your browser, and the engine has an automated test suite you can run yourself.

Use It In Teaching Or Video

Educators and creators are welcome to use AiRA with students or viewers. It is free and has no ads or sign-up. Email me if you want a specific example plan or a walk-through, and tell me what you are making.

What AiRA Is Not

AiRA is an educational tool. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it cannot predict markets.
